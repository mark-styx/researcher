## 1.2 Seeding Unhinged Versions: The Discrediting Operation

**Topics to Investigate:**
- COINTELPRO tactics: FBI operations seeding disinformation within movements (Black Panthers, anti-war left, civil rights organizations) — documented operations from Church Committee
- Operation Mockingbird: what is verified, what is inference — parsing the actual documentary record
- The "limited hangout" technique (coined by John Ehrlichman, Watergate) as a template for controlling narrative
- Parallel construction in law enforcement and its epistemic implications — how manufactured evidence chains corrupt downstream inquiry
- Documented cases where an authentic story was inoculated by association with a ridiculous version: Area 51 (real black programs obscured by UFO noise), MK-Ultra (real program, amplified by implausible extensions), Pizzagate as a possible operation to discredit genuine Epstein-network inquiry
- The "firehose of falsehood" (RAND, 2016) Russian model vs. the more surgical Western model of targeted discrediting
- Astroturfing and front groups: tobacco industry playbook, fossil fuel doubt industry — documented cases where fake grassroots skepticism was manufactured

**Key Questions:**
- Is there evidence that disinformation actors deliberately seed extreme versions of real stories to create an umbrella of embarrassment? Can this be shown beyond inference?
- What is the documented history of the "limited hangout" from Watergate through contemporary intelligence practice?
- How does Pizzagate function epistemically — did it inoculate discussion of documented Epstein-network behavior? Can the timing be reconstructed?
- What is the mechanism by which association with an unhinged claim damages an adjacent legitimate one? Is there psychological research on this (guilt-by-proximity effects)?
- Are there documented whistleblower accounts of operatives tasked with discrediting operations via amplifying fringe?
